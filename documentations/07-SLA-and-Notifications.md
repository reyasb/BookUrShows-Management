# SLA and Notifications

## SLA

The Movie Ticket Request case uses a simple SLA.

### Goal

1 day from case creation.

### Deadline

2 days from case creation.

### Goal Missed

The case is flagged as approaching its deadline.

### Deadline Missed

The case priority is automatically increased.

No complex escalation path is required.
